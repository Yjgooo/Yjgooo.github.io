Partial Identification of Average Treatment Effect Under Covariate Contamination

Planned submission to CLeaR 2027

When some of the information in a study is inaccurate, how much can we still learn about whether a treatment works? This work uses a separate source of reliable data to establish a range of plausible effects, making the uncertainty in the answer explicit.

Exposure-Aware A/B Testing for Repeated User Interactions

Planned submission to The Web Conference 2027

A feature may affect someone differently the first time they use it than the tenth. This project explores how A/B tests can capture those changes, so a single average does not hide how people respond over time.

Minimally Important Change Estimation Through Interval Censoring

Manuscript in preparation

An improvement in a health score does not always mean a patient feels meaningfully better. This work develops methods to estimate how much change matters to patients, and how that threshold may differ across groups.

A Fast Algorithm for Markowitz Portfolio Optimization

Undergraduate research, UC Santa Barbara

Choosing an investment portfolio means balancing potential returns against risk. This undergraduate project developed a faster way to find that balance under a simplified model of how assets move together, with a proof that the method finds the best solution within that model.
